The trade cycle

Five steps from enquiry to paperwork. You deal with one company throughout.

  1. RFQ

    Send grade, form, tonnage and delivery PIN.

  2. Proforma

    We quote and issue a proforma invoice.

  3. RTGS

    You settle by bank transfer against the proforma.

  4. Mill Dispatch

    The mill ships directly to your site.

  5. Documentation

    Tax invoice, e-way bill and mill test certificate.

B2B payment and settlement terms

All orders are settled by advance corporate bank transfer (RTGS / NEFT / IMPS) against an issued proforma invoice. We do not accept retail payment gateways, credit cards or cash on delivery. Bank details are shared only with your quotation.

About & Compliance

Alloy Aviation Private Limited is an asset-light trading company. We coordinate between steel mills and industrial buyers, and handle the billing and dispatch paperwork in between.

Bill-to-ship-to

The supplier bills us, we bill you, and material ships from the mill to your site with its own e-way bill.

No warehouse

We hold no stock, so quotes are tied to mill availability and your specification.

Bank settlement

Advance RTGS / NEFT / IMPS against a proforma invoice. Bank details are shared with your quotation.
